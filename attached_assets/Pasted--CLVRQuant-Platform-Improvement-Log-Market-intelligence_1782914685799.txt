## Signal engine reliability
**Restored continuous idea generation.** Corrected a fault in the engine's safety
circuit breaker that could keep it in a paused state longer than intended, which
was suppressing new trade ideas for extended periods. The breaker now releases
automatically after a bounded cooldown, so the engine resumes on its own without
manual intervention.
_Status: fix prepared — deploying today._

## Signal integrity
**Directional consistency safeguard.** Added a server-side check so that every
trade idea's entry, stop, and targets are always consistent with its stated
direction (long vs short). If a proposal's levels don't line up with its
direction, the system now automatically corrects the geometry before the idea is
shown — eliminating cases where a short idea could display long-style targets.
_Status: fix prepared — deploying today._

## Transparency & framing
**Standardized how analysis is described.** Model outputs are now presented
consistently as **model-confidence scoring** within a decision-support framework.
Unsubstantiated performance representations have been removed from marketing and
in-app surfaces, and educational-use and risk disclosures remain prominent
throughout.
_Status: in progress._

---

## How ideas are produced (reference — unchanged)
Ideas are generated with multi-factor scoring (market structure, momentum,
funding and open-interest signals, and macro alignment) and pass through
server-side risk controls — volatility-based stops, liquidity checks,
friction-adjusted risk/reward, and macro protection — before anything is
displayed. All content is for informational and educational purposes only; users
are solely responsible for their own trading decisions.
