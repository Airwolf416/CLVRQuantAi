Add a HardTrendFilter as a publisher-stage gate. Runs AFTER archetype 
classification but BEFORE EQS in the publish pipeline. Applies to all 
three signal endpoints (scanner, /analyze, /kronos).

LOGIC

For each candidate signal, compute the trend state on the trade's 
asset using two checks:

1. Daily trend (primary):
   - daily_ema20_slope = sign of (EMA20_today - EMA20_7d_ago) on 
     1D candles
   - If daily_ema20_slope > 0 and price > EMA20: trend = UP
   - If daily_ema20_slope < 0 and price < EMA20: trend = DOWN
   - Otherwise: trend = NEUTRAL

2. Intraday trend (confirmation):
   - hourly_ema20_slope = sign of (EMA20_now - EMA20_24h_ago) on 1H candles
   - Must agree with daily for trend to be considered STRONG

DECISION

- Signal direction must agree with trend OR archetype must be 
  MEAN_REVERSION_EXHAUSTION
- LONG signal in DOWN trend: SUPPRESS unless archetype is 
  MEAN_REVERSION_EXHAUSTION
- SHORT signal in UP trend: SUPPRESS unless archetype is 
  MEAN_REVERSION_EXHAUSTION
- NEUTRAL trend: pass (no filter)

This is the gate the dataset would have most benefited from. 
Counter-trend signals on majors had 0% win rate; this rule kills them 
before publish unless the model is explicitly fading.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Based on the 32-day historical record:
- Estimated suppression rate: 20-30% of signals
- Expected win rate lift on remaining signals: +8 to +12 percentage 
  points
- Sample: counter-trend share was 40.8% on tokens with sufficient 
  volume; not all those are pure counter-trend (some are short-term 
  fades that work), so the filter is more conservative than killing 
  all counter-trend
